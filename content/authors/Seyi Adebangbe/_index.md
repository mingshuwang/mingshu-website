Dr. Seyi Adewale Adebangbe is an environmental scientist and geospatial analyst whose research focuses on climate risks, oil spills, and coastal degradation. He brings over a decade of GIS experience, including four years leading GIS and information management for the UN Migration Agency (IOM). He recently completed his PhD at the University of Glasgow and is a Postdoctoral Research Associate on the RACC project, tackling coastal hazards through geospatial analysis.
